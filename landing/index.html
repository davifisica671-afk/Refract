Questions

Is it really private?

Yes. Transcription happens on your device and the AI runs on your own key. There is no Refract server receiving your conversations.

What does "free" mean?

The app is free. You bring your own LLM API key (or run a local model), so you only pay your provider — often cents per session. A Pro tier adds hosted extras.

Can the other side tell I'm using it?

No. The overlay is invisible to screen-share and meeting software.

Which platforms?

Windows and Linux today; macOS next.
